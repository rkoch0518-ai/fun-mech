Call your giant robot. Swing your arms — it swings its fists. Take on the bosses with your friends.

Meta Quest — coming soon See the game

For Meta Quest 2, Quest Pro, Quest 3 and Quest 3S · Online multiplayer · Voice chat

Your own giant robot

Run around like a gorilla, then call down a mech and climb into its head. Its arms are your arms: push the ground to stomp along, swing to punch.

Eight kinds of mech

Giant, Pincer, Shark, Crystal, Wolf, Gorilla, Dino and Dragon mechs, 20 skins each. GiantPincerSharkCrystalWolfGorillaDinoDragon

Slam open Mech Boxes

Pick up a Mech Box, slam it on the ground and watch the roulette: COMMON, RARE, EPIC or LEGENDARY. Your new mech appears full size, then its figure is yours.

Five bosses

The Shark Boss, the Crab King, the Gorilla King, the Fire Dragon and the Tyrant Rex, each in a world of its own. Watch for their warnings and fight them together.

Grow your mech

Feed your mech the crystals hidden round the forest. Every mech goes up to level 20, and grows bigger and tougher on the way.

Build your own

Mix any head, body, arms and legs at the workbench, paint it, and take it anywhere. Get new parts from Part Boxes and bosses.

Fuse with a friend

Two mechs become one giant fused mech: one of you drives, the other rides and can take the controls.

Pets and gear

A hawk that flies your mech, a pup that bites bosses and a fox that finds crystals. A laser gun, a grappling hook and a hammer for your mech's fist, and arm makeup.

Trade and share

Trade mechs with other players at the trading table, and take photos and videos with the in-game camera.

See it in action

Pictures from the game. Tap one to see it full size.

Play with friends

Jump into a public room, or make your own.

  • Public rooms for up to 10 players
  • Private rooms with a code to share
  • Voice chat with the people in your room
  • Everyone fights the same monsters and bosses

Controls

Grip grabs · hold a mech figure and press Y to call it · hold B + Y to get out
Left Menu button opens the menu: rooms, FUSE, block, mute, report and the mic switch.

A friendly place to play

Online play only works when it feels safe, so the safety tools are part of the game. Come and say hi in our Discord server.

  • Block, mute or report any player from the menu
  • Reports are read by real people, who can ban players from the whole game
  • Private room hosts can kick and ban people from their own room
  • Your microphone has an on/off switch, and you only get a random nickname — no real names
