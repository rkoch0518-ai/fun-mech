Privacy Policy

Fun Mech for Meta Quest · Last updated: November 8, 2026

This policy explains what information Fun Mech (the "game") collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. The game is made by Fun Mech ("we", "us"). You can reach us in our Fun Mech Discord server (ask in the #help channel, or send a message to a moderator).

The short version

  • We do not ask for your real name, address, phone number or payment details. There are no real-money purchases in the game.
  • Your player is identified by a random ID the game makes on your headset and a random nickname (like "Gorilla4821").
  • If you use voice chat, your voice is sent live to the other players in your room. We do not record it.
  • We do not show ads and we do not sell your information.

Microphone

The game asks for permission to use the microphone for voice chat. The microphone only sends sound while you are in an online room and the MIC switch in the Safety menu is on. You can switch it off at any time (left controller Menu button, then MIC), mute any single player, or block them. If you decline the permission, the game works normally without voice. Please do not share personal information in voice chat; other players can hear you.

Who we share information with

We use these services to run the game. Each handles information under its own policy:

  • Photon / Exit Games – online rooms and voice chat. photonengine.com
  • Microsoft PlayFab – game account, coins and what you own, ban and staff lists, report events. privacy.microsoft.com
  • Meta – the game is sold through the Meta Horizon Store, checks ownership with Meta's Platform SDK, and runs on Meta Quest; Meta's own policies apply to your Meta account and headset. meta.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • LIV – the in-game camera. liv.tv
  • Discord – where our moderators read player reports, and our community server (the game's JOIN DISCORD button only opens the invite page). discord.com/privacy
  • Cloudflare – the small relay that passes reports to our moderators. cloudflare.com/privacypolicy

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect players from harm. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

  • Room and voice data is not kept: it only exists while you are in the room.
  • Your game account (ID, coins and what you own) is kept while the game is available, so your progress is there when you return.
  • Reports and ban records are kept as long as needed to keep the game safe.
  • Information on your headset stays there until you clear the game's data or uninstall it.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your data. Send a message to a moderator in our Fun Mech Discord server with your player ID (shown beside the staff room door and on the ban screen) or describe your game account, and we will delete the game account and its records, unless we must keep a ban record to protect other players.
  • Ask what we have. You can ask us for a copy of the information tied to your player ID.
  • Reset on your headset. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in your headset settings, removes the progress stored on the headset and creates a new random player ID next time.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you may have additional rights, such as correcting your information or objecting to how it is used. Contact us and we will help.

Children

The game is not meant for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Meta Quest accounts for younger users are set up and supervised by a parent or guardian under Meta's rules. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

The game has cartoon-style robot fights with monsters and bosses, and online voice chat with other players. Please check the age rating on the store page.

Safety and moderation

Online play has block, mute and report tools in the Safety menu. Reports are read by us and may lead to a warning, a room kick or a ban. Our staff can remove players from a room, and the host of a private room can kick or ban players from that room.

Security

We use reputable services and limit what is collected, but no system is perfectly secure. The random player ID is not a password: do not treat it as private from the players who report or ban you.

Changes

If we change what the game collects, we will update this page and the date at the top. Material changes will be mentioned in the game's store page or updates.
