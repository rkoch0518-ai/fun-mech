Support

Help with Fun Mech on Meta Quest.

Contact us

Ask in the #help channel of our Fun Mech Discord server, or send a message to a moderator there. Please include your headset model and what happened. We usually answer within a few days.

Frequently asked questions

How do I move?

Push your hands against the ground, walls and trees and swing your arms to run and jump, like a gorilla. No sticks needed.

How do I get a mech?

Go out of the south door to the Mech Bay. Touch a mech's green button twice to buy its Mech Box (300 coins). Pick the box up with grip and slam it on the ground to open it. The little figure that comes out is your mech: put it at your left hip to holster it.

How do I call my mech and get out again?

Outside, take the figure from your hip, hold it and press Y (or B). Your mech drops in and you are in its head. Your arms are its arms: push the ground with its hands to walk, and punch. Hold B and Y together to get out.

How do mechs get bigger?

Find the glowing crystals in the forest (monsters drop them too). Close your mech's fist on one and bring it up to its head. Each mech goes up to level 20, and grows every 5 levels: at level 20 it's twice the size.

Where are the bosses?

Follow the paths through the gates in the forest walls: the Shark Boss in Shark Cove, the Crab King on Crab Shore, the Gorilla King in the Gorilla Temple, the Fire Dragon on Volcano Peak and the Tyrant Rex in Dino Valley. Everyone who hurts a boss gets coins, a Mech Box and a Part Box when it falls.

How do I build my own mech?

Stand on the blue pad behind the Mech Bay to go to the Sandbox Yard. At the workbench, pick a head, body, arms and legs and three colours. You start with the Giant mech's parts; more come out of Part Boxes (the kiosk beside the workbench, and bosses). TAKE IT WITH YOU puts its figure in your holster so you can call it anywhere.

How do I fuse with a friend's mech?

Both of you call your mechs. Open the menu (left Menu button), go to FUSE and ask your friend. When they say YES, you become one giant mech. The rider can hold A and X to take the controls. Hold B and Y to split apart.

How do I play with friends?

Press the left Menu button, open ROOMS, then NEW CODE to make a private room. Tell your friends the code. They open ROOMS, type the code and press JOIN CODE.

Voice chat isn't working.

Check three things: the game has microphone permission (Quest Settings → Apps → permissions), the MIC switch in the menu says MIC ON, and you are in an online room. You won't hear anyone you muted or blocked.

How do I report, block or mute someone?

Press the left Menu button. Every other player in the room is listed with MUTE, BLOCK and REPORT buttons. Touch a button with your hand. When you report, pick a reason. Blocked players disappear for you.

I got banned. Can I appeal?

Yes. Send a message to a moderator in our Fun Mech Discord server with your player ID (shown on the ban screen) and a short explanation. Temporary bans end by themselves on the date shown on the ban screen.

Do I need to buy anything?

No. There are no purchases with real money. Coins are earned by beating monsters and bosses, and spent on Mech Boxes, Part Boxes, pets, attachments and makeup.

It says "UPDATE REQUIRED".

A new version of the game is out. Close the game and update it from your Library on the headset.

Will it make me feel sick?

Moving on foot is physical (your arms). Inside a mech you look out from high up and the mech moves with your arms, which some people find more intense. Take breaks, and stop if you feel unwell.

How do I delete my data?

See the privacy policy. Send a message to a moderator on our Discord server and we will delete your game account. Uninstalling the game removes everything stored on your headset.

Safety tips

  • Don't share personal information in voice chat. Other players can hear you.
  • Use the MIC switch in the menu whenever you don't want to talk.
  • Be kind. Reports about harassment, cheating and bad names are read by real people.
  • Make sure you have clear space around you before you play: you will swing your arms.
